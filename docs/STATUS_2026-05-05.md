# Status Report — 2026-05-05

Inverse-MoGP-rBCM pipeline for Herschel-Bulkley parameter estimation.
Snapshot of architecture, what works, what doesn't, and where the
remaining error comes from.

---

## 1. Production architecture (sy-model)

### Surrogate
- **Model**: `Models/v10_yshape_round2_sy/`
- **Construction**: 3-layer hierarchical partition
  - **L1**: GID router on (W, H) → 25 geometry bins
  - **L2**: log-equal-width y₈ bin lookup → 5 length bins per gid
  - **L3**: KMeans on PCA-shape + **σ_y split** for any sub with N≥600
    and log(σ_y+1) span > 2.0 → ~33 subs per gid (vs ~24 in
    pre-σ_y-split `merged` model)
- **Per-sub GP**: ExactGP, Matern 2.5 ARD kernel, trained on log-space
  (log η, log σ_y).  Each sub stores `X_phys`, `state_dict`,
  `val_rel_wrms_median` for downstream trust scoring.

### Inverse loop (production setup1 + setup2)

```bash
# setup1 (single-setup inverse + Hessian-orthogonal proposal of setup2)
python -m Optimization.setup1 \
    --state-root Models/v10_yshape_round2_sy \
    -f data/.../ref_<material>_<H>_<W>_1 --density <ρ> \
    --shape-l2-mode eps --shape-l2-eps-cm 0.5 --shape-eps-cap-by-primary \
    --shape-top-k 10 --shape-weight-mode gp_fit --shape-gpfit-oversample 1 \
    --shape-popsize 8 --shape-max-iter 20

# setup2 (joint inverse with σ_y prior anchored to setup1 result)
python -m Optimization.setup2 \
    --state-root Models/v10_yshape_round2_sy \
    -f <ref_dir_1> -s <ref_dir_2> \
    --shape-l2-mode eps --shape-l2-eps-cm 0.5 --shape-eps-cap-by-primary \
    --shape-top-k 2 --shape-weight-mode gp_fit \
    --shape-warm-start-from-first --shape-sy-prior-weight 2.0 \
    --shape-popsize 8 --shape-max-iter 20
```

### Pre-processing
1. `Calibration/extract_flow_distance.py --dir <ref_dir>`
   → `flow_distances.csv/json` from config_*.png + camera_params.xml
2. `Calibration/build_y_obs.py --dir <ref_dir>`
   → `y_obs.npy` (8 floats, frames 1..8 distance in cm)

### Speed
- setup1: ~60 s (sy-model + p8 i20 + K=10)
- setup2: ~22 s (joint, K=2 + warm-start)
- **Total workflow: ~82 s** per material

## 2. Empirical results

### Chuno (lower viscosity, lower σ_y)

| | n | η | σ_y | RMSE_avg vs real |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **truth** (rheometer) | 0.572 | 13.90 | 11.10 | — |
| setup1 sy + production | 0.645 | 12.86 | 10.70 | 0.260 cm |
| **joint setup2 + warm + sy_prior** ⭐ | 0.645 | 12.86 | **10.70** | **0.260 cm** |

θ-space distance to truth: σ_y −4 %, η −8 %, n +13 %.
**Excellent agreement** — flow curve overlays rheometer almost
perfectly across γ̇ ∈ [1, 100] s⁻¹.

### Okonomiyaki (higher viscosity, higher σ_y, larger surface tension effect)

| | n | η | σ_y |
|---|---|---|---|
| **truth** (rheometer 24°C) | 0.502 | 60.27 | 71.72 |
| setup1 sy + production | 0.377 | 72.33 | **197.98** |

θ-space distance to truth: σ_y **+176 %**, η +20 %, n −25 %.
**Reverse fails** — flow curve shows fake yield-stress plateau at low
γ̇ that doesn't exist in real data.

## 3. Why Okonomiyaki fails — full diff decomposition

At Okonomiyaki setup1 θ̂ = (0.377, 72.33, 197.98) on (W=2.2, H=2.5):

```
y_obs (real):     [0.598, 1.248, 1.715, 1.887, 1.986, 2.066, 2.142, 2.186]
surrogate(θ̂):    [0.470, 1.117, 1.509, 1.630, 1.675, 1.702, 1.720, 1.734]
MPM(θ̂):          [0.491, 1.226, 1.655, 1.754, 1.781, 1.806, 1.827, 1.847]
MPM(truth):       [0.587, 1.540, 2.115, 2.341, 2.456, 2.540, 2.611, 2.672]
```

| Diff | RMSE | Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| MPM(truth) − real | **0.41 cm** | sim > real | **Surface tension gap** (MPM does not model surface tension) |
| MPM(θ̂) − real | 0.21 cm | sim < real | σ_y over-pushed → MPM stops too early |
| surrogate(θ̂) − MPM(θ̂) | 0.11 cm | surrogate < MPM | **GP prediction bias** at z_hat |
| surrogate(θ̂) − real | 0.31 cm | combined | What CMA-ES sees as residual (cannot reduce) |

### Decomposition of σ_y bias (+126 from truth 72 → 198)

```
+126 in σ_y composed of:
  ~ +50    Compensating surface tension gap (~0.41 cm of MPM excess)
  ~ +75    Over-correction caused by GP prediction bias (~0.11 cm)
```

**~60 % of the σ_y bias is attributable to GP imprecision**, not
surface tension itself.  Surface tension contributes ~40 %.

### Why GP is imprecise at z_hat

10 routed subs (Fix A v2 spillover bin_2 ∪ bin_3) at z_hat:

| sub | bin | N | val_rwrms | weight | in_bbox? | σ at z_hat | RMSE vs MPM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | 2 | 282 | 0.046 | 0.198 | ✓ | 0.118 | 0.133 |
| 17 | 3 | 289 | 0.005 | 0.171 | ✗ | 0.203 | **0.460** |
| 31 | 3 | 289 | 0.026 | 0.166 | ✓ | 0.242 | **0.044** |
| 15 | 3 | 388 | 0.013 | 0.125 | ✗ | 0.290 | 0.211 |
| 11 | 2 | 601 | 0.024 | 0.090 | ✗ | 0.204 | 0.319 |
| 30 | 2 | 282 | 0.076 | 0.083 | ✗ | 0.246 | 0.257 |
| 28 | 2 | 600 | 0.034 | 0.070 | ✓ | 0.122 | 0.397 |
| 12 | 2 | 347 | 0.053 | 0.049 | ✓ | 0.157 | 0.498 |
| 10 | 2 | 355 | 0.031 | 0.024 | ✗ | 0.321 | 0.819 |
| 27 | 2 | 355 | 0.042 | 0.023 | ✗ | 0.230 | 0.779 |

Three failure modes:

**(a) 6/10 subs are extrapolating** at z_hat (z_hat outside training
bbox). GPs in extrapolation rely mainly on prior; mean prediction
unreliable.

**(b) Weight-actual-error decoupling**: gp_fit weights were locked at
routing time using GP fit at z_nn (each sub's own training nearest
neighbour to y_obs). After CMA-ES walks to z_hat, the fixed weights
don't reflect actual predictive accuracy. Example: sub_17 (weight
0.171 → second highest) actually has RMSE 0.460 cm at z_hat; sub_31
(weight 0.166 → essentially the same) has RMSE 0.044 cm.

**(c) GP variance σ poorly calibrated to actual error**:
- sub_31: σ=0.24 (high → "I'm uncertain"), RMSE=0.04 (actually accurate)
- sub_28: σ=0.12 (low → "confident"), RMSE=0.40 (wrong)
- Spearman correlation σ vs RMSE ≈ 0.5–0.6 (weak positive)

Implication: BLUE-style 1/σ² weighting and proper Bayesian MoG
likelihood both **fail empirically** because they trust σ. Tested
both — BLUE made σ_y worse on Chuno (10.7 → 5.0 floor); MoG
likelihood collapsed both materials to wrong basins.

### sub_31 specifically (user-asked)

- N = 289 (small relative to other subs in bin)
- z_hat = (0.377, log 72.3, log 198.0) lies at (−1.71σ, +0.89σ,
  +1.79σ) from sub_31's training centroid
- Only 16/289 = 5.5 % of sub_31's training points within 1σ of z_hat
- GP variance correctly inflates (σ=0.24, top-3 highest)
- But mean prediction extrapolates to RMSE 0.044 (lowest of all 10)
- → "**Over-cautious uncertainty quantification**": GP knows it's
  extrapolating but happens to extrapolate well thanks to function
  smoothness

## 4. Math improvements tested (all opt-in flags, none default-on)

| # | Method | Flag | On Okono | On Chuno | Net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Fix A v2** (eps_cap_by_primary) | `--shape-eps-cap-by-primary` | n escapes floor (0.30→0.38), σ_y improves | no-op (symmetric bins) | **DEFAULT BASELINE** ✅ |
| 1 | BLUE inverse-variance weighting | `--shape-weight-mode inv_var` | n closer / σ_y worse | σ_y collapses to floor | reverted (opt-in only) |
| 2 | Hessian credible interval | (not implemented) | post-hoc, doesn't change θ̂ | same | skipped |
| 3 | Multi-start cluster diagnostic | `--shape-cma-seed-offset N` | 5 seeds → essentially identical θ̂ → CMA-ES is robust | (not tested) | useful as diagnostic, doesn't improve θ̂ |
| 4 | Soft binning attention | `--shape-l2-mode soft_attn` | collapsed to bin_2 dominance | (not tested) | reverted (opt-in only) |
| 5 | Info-weighted frame_w | `--shape-info-frame-w` | marginally worse | (not tested) | reverted (opt-in only) |
| 6 | Per-sub z_scale bbox padding | `--shape-per-sub-z-scale` | no change | (not tested) | reverted (opt-in only) |
| 7 | Mixture-of-GPs Bayesian likelihood | `--loss-mode mog_likelihood` | z² distance 1.15 → 4.20 ❌ | z² 0.18 → 0.27 ❌ | reverted (opt-in only) |

Combination experiments:
- Fix A v2 + (1) BLUE: net z² slightly better on Okono (1.08 vs 1.15)
  but **Chuno σ_y collapsed** (10.7 → 5.0). Not safe to default on.
- Fix A v2 + (1)+(5)+(6) stacked: z² 2.05 (worse). Stacking
  compounded errors.
- soft_attn + (1): z² 2.15. New routing destabilises.

**Conclusion**: no math-layer improvement gives a universal win.
Production stays at Fix A v2 only.

## 5. The fundamental dilemma

For any single (n, η, σ_y), define:
- `θ_truth` — physical HB params (rheometer measurement)
- `MPM(θ)` — simulator output (no surface tension)
- `Real(θ_truth) = MPM(θ_truth) + Δ_surface_tension(W, H, θ_truth)`

CMA-ES solves:
```
θ̂ = argmin_θ ‖ surrogate(θ) − Real(θ_truth) ‖
```

The gradient implicitly pulls θ̂ to compensate for both:
- `Δ_surface_tension` (can't be removed without modelling it in MPM)
- `surrogate − MPM` GP error (could be reduced by better surrogate)

When Δ_surface_tension is large relative to y_obs (Okonomiyaki:
22 % vs Chuno 11 %), σ_y inflates to compensate. When GP additionally
mis-predicts MPM, σ_y over-corrects further (Okonomiyaki: ~75 of the
+126 σ_y bias).

## 6. Why prior-work snapdiff achieves better θ̂ with same MPM

(For reference; not pursued in current work to keep workflow fast.)

| Factor | Current (8-frame distance) | Prior work (silhouette snapdiff) |
|---|---|---|
| Information per experiment | 8 scalar floats | ~4 M binary pixels |
| Surface tension placement | Front edge → 100 % of measurement | Front edge → ~1 % of pixels (averaged with bulk) |
| Forward model in inverse | Surrogate (GP) | MPM directly |
| Loss landscape | Ridge-degenerate | Smooth single-mode |

Each factor contributes to snapdiff's better θ accuracy. Trade-off:
snapdiff inverse takes hours; current takes seconds.

## 7. Open issues / future work (ranked)

1. **GP calibration** (σ ≈ actual RMSE): per-sub hold-out validation
   when training, store σ correction factor. Would unlock BLUE/MoG.
2. **Active learning** in θ regions of low density (esp. Okonomiyaki
   regime n≈0.5, η≈60, σ_y≈70). Add ~200 MPM samples and re-train
   the affected sub-GPs.
3. **Surface-tension correction in MPM** (months of dev): fix the
   gap at the source rather than compensating in θ.
4. **Sim-vs-real residual GP** (Kennedy-O'Hagan model discrepancy):
   one-time calibration on 3–5 known materials, applied as a fixed
   y_obs correction at inference.
